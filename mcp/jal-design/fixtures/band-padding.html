Base band

No tone of its own.

Heading on the edge

Then a long empty band.

Base

Between tones.

Lopsided

More room below than above.

Base

Between tones.

Even band

One rhythm above and below.

Base

Between tones.

Merged one

Same tone as the next.

Merged two

One gap between them.

Base, uneven, no tone

Not a tone band.
